# CHORUS

One goal, coordinated conversations. A browser conductor creates an event-relocation or equipment-and-delivery mission. The user reviews contacts and explicitly approves research calls. Phone agents gather sourced terms; they cannot spend money or expand permissions. Shared briefs expose new evidence, current constraints and negotiation requests across calls. A revision invalidates plan approval. A feasible plan is still a proposal, never a reservation.

Flagship: 40 people, 18:00 Asia/Kolkata, INR 12,000 additional spend. Venue access at 17:30 and catering setup of 45 minutes conflict. Investigate earlier access, then lower budget to INR 9,000 and reevaluate. Configurable business terms can leave the mission blocked. Second scenario: equipment quantity/capacity and delivery readiness before an event.

Simulation is explicit throughout. It uses durable calls, tools and engine events, with configurable responses, no generated fake transcript or audio. Live voice uses Bolna, when configured. Spoken revisions are mediated by the conductor's tool calls; active phone agents obtain changes on their next brief refresh. Pause blocks dispatch and plan approval. Active calls may finish; no undocumented interruption capability is claimed.
