# CHORUS

One goal, coordinated conversations. A browser conductor creates an event-relocation or equipment-and-delivery mission. The user reviews contacts and explicitly approves research calls. Phone agents gather sourced terms; they cannot spend money or expand permissions. Shared briefs expose new evidence, current constraints and negotiation requests across calls. A revision invalidates plan approval. A feasible plan is still a proposal, never a reservation.

The public landing page at / introduces the product with an interactive, explicitly illustrative mission example. The authenticated workspace lives at /app; old mission URLs remain compatible. Mission control explains the next step, keeps approvals reachable, supports mobile mission switching and exports a sourced brief. Its conductor is a side panel on desktop and a full-height sheet on mobile with a fixed composer.

Recovery preserves intent and provenance: interrupted messages keep their text and retry the same command, stale edits require review of the latest revision, and failed refreshes show the last saved view. Missing or stalled speech always leaves typed controls usable. A manual task retry invalidates old terms and requires fresh research approval. The app never turns a failed or uncertain provider dispatch into an automatic redial.

Flagship: 40 people, 18:00 Asia/Kolkata, INR 12,000 additional spend. Venue access at 17:30 and catering setup of 45 minutes conflict. Investigate earlier access, then lower budget to INR 9,000 and reevaluate. Configurable business terms can leave the mission blocked. Second scenario: equipment quantity/capacity and delivery readiness before an event.

Simulation is explicit throughout. It uses durable attempts, tools and engine events, with configurable responses, no generated fake transcript or audio. Browser voice and typed commands create either mission template, revise supported constraints, report status, pause/resume, request research approval and retry a task. Browser speech recognition requires support and permission; typed fallback always remains available. Optional browser speech synthesis reads the actual reply. The recognized text and reply persist across refresh. No phone number is needed for these flows; operator email/password login is sufficient.

Live voice uses Bolna, when configured. Spoken revisions are mediated by the conductor's tool calls; active phone agents obtain changes on their next brief refresh. Pause blocks dispatch and plan approval. Active calls may finish; no undocumented interruption capability is claimed. Business discovery, general-purpose planning and binding purchases/reservations are outside this version. Actual Bolna execution and hosted deployment are separate unverified milestones, not prerequisites for trying the complete simulation workflow.
