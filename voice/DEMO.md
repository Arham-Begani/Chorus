# Demonstration script

## Three-minute simulation rehearsal

1. Open mission control and point to **SIMULATION — no phone calls are placed**. State that the same persisted coordination engine also serves the Bolna adapter; live verification remains separate.
2. Create Event relocation. Show 40 people, tomorrow at 18:00 Asia/Kolkata, INR 12,000 additional spending. Authorize simulated research. Three persisted attempts start together.
3. Open evidence as terms arrive. Venue initially offers 17:30 access; caterer needs 45 minutes. Show the 15-minute setup conflict. The next venue brief includes the caterer's fact and asks for earlier access. The worker investigates without claiming instant interruption or a reservation.
4. Show 17:00 access and the feasible proposed plan. Before approving, revise the budget to INR 9,000. Point to revision 2 and stale previous offers/approval. Approve research for this revision. The engine checks alternative terms and either finds a qualifying plan or explains remaining blockers.
5. Refresh. Pause and restart the worker; resume. Mission and attempt identities persist. Open the evidence drawer: source attempts, simulation labels, confirmation status and revision are visible.
6. Approve the proposed plan and say explicitly: "This records a preferred plan; nothing has been purchased or booked."

## Failure and reuse rehearsal

Create another mission; before authorization set every business's responses to `[]`. Authorize research: calls return refusals and the mission blocks with missing terms, rather than fabricated success. Then create Equipment rental & delivery, authorize simulation, and show the same capacity/readiness/setup checks producing a plan.
