# Demonstration script

## Three-minute simulation rehearsal

1. Open the landing page, explore **Resolve timing** and **Revise budget** in the explicitly illustrative example, then choose **Open workspace**. In mission control point to **SIMULATION — no phone calls are placed**. The persisted coordination engine also serves the Bolna adapter; live verification remains separate.
2. Open **Talk to conductor**. Speak, or type if browser recognition is unavailable: “Our venue cancelled. Find a venue for forty people tomorrow at six pm under twelve thousand rupees.” Show the parsed deadline/budget and persisted conductor conversation. Close the dialog and authorize simulated research. Three persisted attempts start together; no telephone number is needed.
3. Open evidence as terms arrive. Venue initially offers 17:30 access; caterer needs 45 minutes. Show the 15-minute setup conflict. The next venue brief includes the caterer's fact and asks for earlier access. The worker investigates without claiming instant interruption or a reservation.
4. Show 17:00 access and the feasible proposed plan. Before approving, open the conductor and say or type “Lower the budget to nine thousand rupees.” Point to revision 2 and stale previous offers/approval. Approve research for this revision. The engine checks alternative terms and either finds a qualifying plan or explains remaining blockers. Ask “What is blocking the plan?” for a reply based on persisted constraints/evidence.
5. Say or type “Pause”, refresh and reopen the conductor: its history persists. Resume. Separately restart the worker to demonstrate recovery; mission and attempt identities persist. Open the evidence drawer: source attempts, simulation labels, confirmation status and revision are visible.
6. Approve the proposed plan and say explicitly: "This records a preferred plan; nothing has been purchased or booked."

## Failure and reuse rehearsal

Create another mission; before authorization set every business's responses to `[]`. Authorize research: calls return refusals and the mission blocks with missing terms, rather than fabricated success. Then create Equipment rental & delivery, authorize simulation, and show the same capacity/readiness/setup checks producing a plan.

Private participant role cards are supplied as an ignored local file, judge-role-cards.private.md; they are not published in the public repository.

## UX and recovery rehearsal

Show **Your next step** and use its research-review action to reach the approval card. Open the conductor on mobile: the composer and close control stay visible while history scrolls. Fill a suggested command, then send it. Export the brief and show constraints, missing/current terms and source attempt references. Switch missions on mobile and return through a saved URL. If the connection drops, keep the cached view visible and reconnect; a lost conductor response retries the original command without duplicating the mission. Speech denial or a stalled speech service leaves typed input available. Provider calls are not involved in these demonstrations.
